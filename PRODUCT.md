# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows users who repeatedly copy text, images, snippets, and credentials and want a fast, local place to retrieve, organize, and reuse them without breaking their current workflow.

## Product Purpose

Clipboard is a local-first clipboard manager for capturing, searching, organizing, previewing, and reusing clipboard content. Success means common clipboard tasks remain immediate, predictable, and recoverable while sensitive data stays on the user's device.

## Brand Personality

Calm, pragmatic, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused desktop utility with restrained editorial warmth, not a promotional surface.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the app into a card-heavy SaaS dashboard, decorative glass interface, or novelty productivity tool. Do not introduce visual patterns that conflict with the screenshots in `/reference`.

## Design Principles

- Preserve familiar desktop interactions and keep the tool out of the user's way.
- Treat local data, especially passwords, as sensitive and make destructive actions explicit.
- Keep information dense, legible, and visually consistent across all four product areas.
- Add feedback and recovery paths for states that can fail without obscuring the main task.
- Match the reference screenshots before introducing any new visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep all core actions keyboard accessible with visible focus states, semantic labels, and reduced-motion-safe behavior. Maintain readable contrast and do not encode state by color alone.
